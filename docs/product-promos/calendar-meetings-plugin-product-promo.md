# Calendar & Meetings Plugin

Client-facing product promo and feature summary

Prepared: June 22, 2026

## Product Positioning

The Calendar & Meetings plugin is the meeting operations layer for WebXpanse and Clarity CRM. It helps a business owner turn scheduled calls, customer conversations, meeting notes, and follow-up decisions into useful workspace memory.

This plugin is not just a calendar screen. It connects calendar sync, meeting bot setup, meeting note ingestion, meeting prep, and CRM follow-up context so the team can understand what happened before, during, and after an important conversation.

For a client, the value is simple: Calendar & Meetings helps turn appointments into action.

## Executive Summary

Calendar & Meetings helps small teams run meeting-heavy work with more consistency. It brings the schedule into the CRM, prepares the team before calls, supports meeting bot and note workflows, and helps the system turn meeting context into notes, tasks, contact updates, deal movement, and better AI guidance.

The plugin is designed for entrepreneurs, sales teams, customer success teams, onboarding teams, delivery teams, and account managers who rely on conversations to move the business forward. In many small businesses, the most important decisions happen during calls, but the follow-up often lives in memory, chat messages, loose notes, or one person's inbox. Calendar & Meetings helps fix that.

It gives the workspace a central place to connect Google or Outlook calendars, review CRM events, import or export calendar files, configure a meeting assistant, manage transcript and consent expectations, ingest meeting notes, and support one-click meeting prep.

The result is not "another calendar." The result is cleaner follow-through after every important customer conversation.

## What Calendar & Meetings Is

Calendar & Meetings is an installable plugin inside WebXpanse and Clarity CRM.

It brings four related jobs into one workspace module:

- Calendar Sync: connect the CRM calendar with Google Calendar or Outlook.
- Meeting Prep: summarize useful contact, deal, note, activity, and communication context before a call.
- Meeting Bot: configure meeting assistant rules, provider settings, join policy, recording mode, transcript requirements, and consent notice.
- Meeting Note Ingestion: turn meeting notes, transcripts, or summaries into CRM context, follow-up tasks, contact updates, and deal signals.

Together, these pieces help the system understand meetings as part of the customer relationship, not as separate calendar appointments.

## Salient Features

Calendar & Meetings is built around practical operating features that support real customer work.

### 1. CRM Calendar View

The plugin unlocks the CRM calendar page for installed workspaces.

The calendar supports month, week, and day views so a team can scan the schedule at the level that makes sense. Users can move to the previous period, the next period, or today's date. Events can be reviewed by day, week, or full month.

This matters because a business owner needs a clear view of what is happening, not just a list of disconnected tasks.

### 2. Event Types And Visual Organization

Calendar events can be organized by event type, including meetings, calls, emails, tasks, and other events.

The system gives these event types different visual colors, making it easier to understand the week at a glance. For example, calls, meetings, tasks, and email-related events can be scanned quickly without opening every item.

This helps busy users spot what kind of work is ahead.

### 3. Owner And Team Filters

The calendar can filter events by assigned user. A person can focus on their own work, while users with broader permission can view all users.

This supports both personal productivity and team management.

For a founder, that means they can ask:

- What do I need to handle today?
- What is the team working on this week?
- Which meetings belong to which person?
- Is follow-up coverage clear?

### 4. Google And Outlook Calendar Connections

The plugin supports connecting Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar through the Calendar setup tab.

This gives the workspace a path to bring outside calendar events into the CRM and keep CRM events connected to the calendars people already use.

The setup page shows existing connected calendars, sync status, provider, calendar name, sync direction, and last sync time.

### 5. Sync Direction Control

Calendar integrations support sync direction options.

The workspace can choose whether sync should work both ways, from the CRM to the external calendar, or from the external calendar into the CRM.

That matters because every business has a different comfort level. Some teams want the CRM to reflect outside events. Others want CRM-created events pushed outward. Some want both.

### 6. iCal Import And Export

The calendar supports importing and exporting iCal files.

Exporting helps users share or preserve CRM calendar events. Importing helps bring event data into the system when a direct provider connection is not the right path.

This keeps the plugin useful even when a team is not ready for a full live calendar integration.

### 7. Calendar Sync Worker

The system includes a worker path for calendar sync, designed to run on a schedule.

This helps the calendar stay current without asking the user to manually refresh every event. In practical terms, it supports the goal of reducing manual calendar administration.

### 8. Meeting Prep

Meeting Prep gives the user a quick summary before a customer call.

It can use contact details, active deals, focus deal context, recent notes, activities, and communications to produce a useful pre-meeting brief. If AI output is not available, the system can still produce a fallback summary from the CRM context.

Meeting prep can include:

- A short summary.
- Key points.
- Open questions.
- Suggested topics.
- Deal context.
- Recent activity.
- Recent communication channels.

This helps the team show up prepared instead of asking the customer to repeat everything.

### 9. Meeting Bot Configuration

The plugin includes meeting bot setup for teams that want automated meeting support.

The configuration can include:

- Bot enabled or disabled.
- Provider selection, such as Zoom or Google Meet support paths.
- Bot display name.
- Join policy.
- Recording mode.
- Transcript requirement.
- Auto-apply mode.
- Consent notice.
- Provider credentials and secrets.
- Webhook and scheduling secrets.

This gives the team a controlled way to prepare meeting automation before relying on it in live customer calls.

### 10. Join Policy

The meeting bot can be governed by a join policy.

The workspace can keep the bot manual, calendar-suggested, or eligible for more automatic joining depending on setup and policy. This is important because meeting automation should match the team's customer experience and consent expectations.

The system is designed so the business can start conservatively and expand later.

### 11. Recording And Transcript Readiness

Calendar & Meetings treats transcript capture as an operational responsibility, not a casual feature.

The plugin includes settings for recording mode and transcript requirements. It also stores a consent notice so the workspace can communicate meeting capture expectations clearly.

This is important for client trust. A business should not surprise customers with meeting capture behavior.

### 12. Meeting Bot Runs

The plugin can track recent meeting bot runs.

A run can show whether a meeting was scheduled, joined, recorded, completed, blocked, failed, or waiting for transcript processing. This gives admins and operators a way to understand what happened instead of guessing.

For a business, this creates visibility. If the bot did not capture a meeting, the team can see that and decide what to do next.

### 13. Meeting Note Ingestion

Meeting Note Ingestion lets the workspace submit meeting notes, transcripts, or summaries into the CRM.

The system can normalize the payload, match the meeting to a contact, match an active deal when possible, analyze the content, and decide what CRM actions are allowed.

This is where the meeting becomes useful business memory.

### 14. Contact And Deal Notes

When meeting notes are accepted, the system can create notes on the matched contact and, when appropriate, the matched deal.

This keeps important conversation details connected to the customer record.

It also helps future users understand what was discussed without needing to search through someone's personal notes.

### 15. Follow-Up Task Creation

Meeting notes can create tasks from action items.

For example, if a transcript or summary includes a next step, the system can create a task linked to the contact, assign it to the owner, set a due date when available, and include source metadata.

This turns "we should follow up" into tracked work.

### 16. Contact Updates With Safety Rules

The plugin can support contact updates from meeting content, but with important safety rules.

Allowed contact fields are controlled by configuration. Confidence thresholds protect against weak or uncertain updates. The system can also run in an additive-only mode, meaning it will avoid overwriting existing contact values when the field already has information.

That is human-friendly automation. It helps the system learn without carelessly changing important records.

### 17. Deal Stage Signals

When a meeting gives strong evidence that a deal has moved forward, the system can suggest or apply a deal stage transition depending on configuration and confidence.

The default idea is careful: deal movement should require enough confidence and the right auto-apply mode. If the workspace is not ready for automatic movement, the system can require review.

This keeps sales progress connected to real conversation evidence.

### 18. Review Modes And Human Control

The plugin supports different automation modes, including suggest-only, safer automatic modes, and fuller automatic modes.

This matters because every client has a different comfort level with AI. A new team may start with review required. A more mature team may allow safe task creation. A highly configured team may allow stronger automation once confidence and policies are clear.

The intention is not to remove human judgment. The intention is to make human judgment easier.

### 19. Workspace Permissions

Calendar and meeting settings are permission-aware.

Different users may have different rights for calendar settings, meeting bot settings, note taker settings, scheduling, and viewing runs. This protects the workspace from casual misconfiguration.

In a client-facing explanation, this means: the right people can manage the system, while ordinary users can focus on their work.

### 20. Marketplace Readiness And Setup Guidance

Calendar & Meetings appears in the Marketplace as an installable module with setup guidance, prerequisites, recommendations, and readiness checks.

The setup path encourages the team to:

- Install the plugin.
- Connect Google or Outlook Calendar.
- Configure meeting bot identity, provider, and join policy when needed.
- Confirm note ingestion readiness.
- Confirm consent and review expectations before relying on meeting capture.

This helps a client roll out the plugin in a sensible order.

## What It Does Not Do

A clear product promise should also be honest about limits.

Calendar & Meetings does not replace human judgment. It does not remove the need for consent, meeting etiquette, or workspace policy. It does not make provider credentials unnecessary. It does not guarantee every transcript will be perfect. It does not mean every suggested contact update or deal movement should be trusted without review.

Instead, it gives the workspace a safer structure:

- Connect the calendar.
- Set policy.
- Prepare for calls.
- Capture meeting context.
- Review or automate within limits.
- Keep the CRM current.
