# Email Plugin

Client-facing product promo and feature summary

Prepared: June 23, 2026

## Product Positioning

The Email plugin is the communication foundation for WebXpanse and Clarity CRM. It gives a workspace a trusted place to set up sender identities, connect outbound email, optionally capture inbound email, manage templates, use signatures, process email queues, and check readiness before the team relies on email workflows.

This plugin is not the Email Assistant. Email Assistant helps with AI drafting, replies, instructions, and digests after the email channel is ready. The Email plugin comes first. It makes sure the business can send and, when needed, receive email through the CRM in a controlled way.

For a client, the value is simple: Email turns customer follow-up from a scattered inbox habit into a reliable business channel.

## Executive Summary

The Email plugin helps email-led businesses create a dependable customer communication system. It supports outreach sender setup, nurture sender setup, SMTP sending, optional IMAP inbox capture, readiness checks, email templates, email signatures, queued delivery, scheduled sends, bulk email workflows, email activity records, and import auditing.

Most businesses already depend on email. Prospects ask questions by email. Customers reply with documents. Owners send quotes, invoices, reminders, follow-ups, welcome messages, and meeting notes. The problem is not that email exists. The problem is that email often lives outside the operating system of the business.

The Email plugin brings email into the workspace. It gives admins a clear setup path, lets users compose and send from CRM context, helps teams reuse approved templates, keeps signatures consistent, queues messages for safer delivery, and checks whether the channel is ready before automation or assistant features depend on it.

The result is not "more email features." The result is a more reliable follow-up engine for the business.

Inbound customer messages may arrive, but the CRM does not always capture them.

Without a reliable email channel, the business faces common problems:

- Follow-up depends too much on memory.
- Teams send from the wrong identity.
- Nurture messages mix with one-off outreach.

- Bulk emails are harder to preview and control.
- Scheduled email depends on manual reminders.
- Email workflows fail because setup was never tested.
- AI email tools are added before the basic channel is trustworthy.

The Email plugin solves this by making the channel visible, testable, and connected to the CRM.

## What The Email Plugin Is

The Email plugin is an installable core communication plugin inside WebXpanse and Clarity CRM.

It owns the email channel setup for the workspace. It handles the practical foundation that other email tools need: sender identities, SMTP, optional IMAP, provider readiness, templates, signatures, queued sends, scheduled sends, activity records, tracking, and health checks.

In simple terms, it gives the workspace:

- A place to configure email sending.
- A place to configure optional email receiving.
- A way to check whether email is ready.
- A way to send individual and bulk email from CRM context.
- A template system for repeatable messaging.
- A signature system for consistent sender presentation.
- A queue so email can be processed in the background.
- Activity and audit surfaces so admins can see what happened.

## What The Email Plugin Is Not

The Email plugin should be positioned clearly.

It is not the Email Assistant. It does not replace the AI drafting and reply workflows handled by Email Assistant. It does not guarantee deliverability if the business has poor domain reputation, invalid credentials, provider limits, or bad recipient lists. It does not bypass email laws, consent rules, unsubscribe expectations, or provider sending limits.

It is the foundation.

That is why it matters. A business should make email dependable before putting automation, bulk sends, nurture workflows, or assistant tools on top of it.

## Salient Features

### 1. Marketplace Setup

The Email plugin is managed from the Marketplace as a core communication plugin. This gives owners and admins a single, visible place to understand whether email is installed, configured, and ready.

Client value:

- Clear onboarding.
- Less confusion about where email setup lives.
- A stronger foundation before email workflows go live.

### 2. Required Core Channel

The plugin is marked as a required core communication module. That means it is treated as a protected foundation for email-led workspaces.

Client value:

- The workspace treats email as infrastructure, not an optional side feature.
- Other tools can depend on a stable email foundation.
- Email Assistant and automation can wait until the channel is ready.

### 3. Outreach Email Identity

The Marketplace setup includes an Outreach tab. This identity is for sales, prospecting, and direct follow-up where the business needs a clear outbound sender.

Client value:

- Sales messages can come from the right address and name.
- Outreach is easier to separate from general system email.
- The team can test outreach readiness before sending real messages.

### 4. Nurture Email Identity

The Marketplace setup also includes a Nurture tab. This identity supports lifecycle follow-up, onboarding, re-engagement, and longer-term relationship messages.

Client value:

- Nurture communication can have its own sender identity.
- Follow-up campaigns feel more organized.
- The business avoids mixing every type of message under one sender.

### 5. Main Email Awareness

The readiness system also considers main email setup when available. Main, outreach, and nurture email can all contribute to email runtime readiness.

Client value:

- The workspace can become ready through a valid email identity.
- Admins can understand which email path is working.
- Existing main email setup can support the broader communication runtime.

### 6. SMTP Sending Setup

Admins can save SMTP host, port, username, password, sender email, sender name, and encryption settings. This is the core setup for sending email through a mail provider.

Client value:

- The CRM can send email through the business's chosen provider.
- Sender identity is explicit.
- Missing credentials are easier to identify.

### 7. Optional IMAP Inbox Capture

Admins can enable IMAP and save inbox settings, including IMAP host, port, username, password, encryption, and folder. This allows the CRM to capture inbound email when the business wants incoming customer messages connected to the system.

Client value:

- Customer replies can become part of the CRM context.
- Inbound email does not have to stay hidden in one person's inbox.
- The team can choose inbox capture only when it is needed.

### 8. OAuth And Platform Default Awareness

The provider summary supports manual SMTP and IMAP, and it can recognize configured mailbox providers such as Gmail or Google Workspace when those are active in the platform. Platform defaults can also support setup when no workspace-specific integration is active.

Client value:

- The system can fit different setup maturity levels.
- Admins can see which provider path is active.
- The business can move from manual setup to connected providers when available.

### 9. Readiness Checks

The Email plugin includes readiness checks for main email, outreach email, nurture email, and whether at least one identity is ready. A channel test can pass, warn, or show that setup is still needed.

Client value:

- The team can test before relying on email.
- Setup blockers are visible.
- Admins can avoid launching workflows on broken credentials.

### 10. Health Labels

The channel health layer gives human-readable labels such as Email not connected, Sending ready, inbox not connected, SMTP and IMAP connected, Gmail connected, or Mailbox connected.

Client value:

- Owners do not need to understand every technical detail.
- Support conversations become easier.
- Teams know whether they can send, receive, or both.

### 11. Communication Runtime Gate

Some pages and workflows enforce communication readiness. If Email or WhatsApp is not ready, the system can redirect a manager to setup or tell non-admin users that communication setup is required.

Client value:

- Users are protected from broken send experiences.
- Owners get guided to the correct setup page.
- The system encourages readiness before action.

### 12. Email Compose Page

The compose page lets users send email from inside the CRM. It can use contacts, templates, draft helpers, signatures, and attachable CRM documents such as quotes, proformas, and invoices.

Client value:

- Users can send from customer context.
- Important documents can move with the conversation.
- Follow-up becomes part of the CRM record.

### 13. Bulk Email Page

The bulk email page supports sending to multiple contacts by tag, stage, assignment, search, company, or selected contacts. It includes preview, compose, template selection, live preview, optional scheduling, and controlled send action.

Client value:

- The business can reach groups without leaving the CRM.
- Recipient selection is more deliberate.
- The team can preview before sending.

### 14. Template Use

The Email plugin works with email templates. Templates can include subjects, HTML body, plain text body, categories, variables, and active status.

Client value:

- The team saves time.
- Messaging becomes more consistent.
- Common follow-up messages do not need to be rewritten every time.

### 15. Template Variables

Templates can use variables such as first name, last name, email, phone, company, and contact ID. The send flow replaces variables with customer-specific information.

Client value:

- Messages can feel personal without being manually rewritten.
- Teams reduce copy-paste mistakes.
- Bulk sending can still carry useful customer context.

### 16. Template Library

The product includes a template library surface. This supports reusable messaging assets that can be reviewed, copied, edited, and used by the workspace.

Client value:

- Teams can start from proven message structures.
- New users do not start from a blank page.
- Businesses can create a shared language for follow-up.

### 17. Email Signatures

Users can create signatures, edit them, preview them, set a default signature, and use them in email workflows.

Client value:

- The business looks more professional.
- Sender information stays consistent.
- Users do not need to manually paste signatures into every email.

### 18. Queued Sending

When email is sent through the service, it is recorded and placed into an email queue unless immediate sending is requested. The queue tracks pending, processing, completed, and failed states.

Client value:

- Email sending becomes more reliable.
- The system can retry instead of losing work.
- Admins can understand whether a message is waiting, processing, completed, or failed.

### 19. Background Email Worker

The background email worker processes queued emails. It also looks for pending emails that may not have a queue row, so fewer messages get stuck.

Client value:

- Sending can happen behind the scenes.
- The user does not have to manually manage every delivery attempt.
- Operational reliability improves.

### 20. Scheduled Email Worker

Scheduled email support allows emails to wait until their scheduled time. The scheduled worker checks due messages and processes them.

Client value:

- Follow-up can be timed.
- Teams can prepare messages earlier.
- Owners can build a communication rhythm without relying on memory.

### 21. Email Tracking Records

The email tables support statuses such as pending, sent, delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, and failed. Tracking records can capture open and click events.

Client value:

- The business can see whether messages are moving.
- Follow-up can be guided by activity.
- Owners get more visibility than a normal sent folder.

### 22. Inbound Email Fetching

When inbound setup is enabled, the fetch worker can connect to configured mailboxes, fetch new email, process messages, and connect activity to CRM records.

Client value:

- Customer replies can enter the operating system.
- The CRM becomes a better source of customer history.
- Teams reduce the gap between inbox activity and follow-up action.

### 23. Import Audit

The Email Import Audit page shows skipped, processed, auto-created, and failed import outcomes. It includes filters for outcome, sender email, reason code, and date range.

Client value:

- Admins can investigate why messages were skipped.
- Inbound capture becomes explainable.
- The team can improve configuration instead of guessing.

### 24. Auto-Created Contact Support

Inbound processing can auto-create customer context when a new sender is processed successfully, depending on the import path and workspace rules.

Client value:

- New customer conversations are less likely to disappear.
- The CRM can become more complete over time.
- Inbound email can support cleaner contact history.

### 25. Sender Profile Selection

The sending layer understands sender profiles such as outreach and nurture. Nurture messages can require a ready nurture sender before they send.

Client value:

- Different business motions can use different identities.
- Campaign and nurture sends can be protected from wrong-sender mistakes.
- Setup requirements are enforced where they matter.

### 26. Template Usage Tracking

When templates are used through the send service, usage count can be incremented. This gives the system a way to understand which templates are actually being used.

Client value:

- Teams can learn which messages matter.
- Poorly used templates can be improved or retired.
- The business can build a better message library over time.

### 27. Workspace-Safe Processing

The workers run with workspace context. This helps queued and scheduled email actions process under the correct workspace.

Client value:

- Multi-workspace operations are safer.
- Email activity stays connected to the right business.
- The system avoids mixing records across clients or workspaces.

### 28. Readiness Before Automation

The Email plugin is intentionally separate from assistant automation. It prepares the channel so Email Assistant, workflow automation, bulk campaigns, nurture sequences, and customer follow-up tools have a dependable base.

Client value:

- Automation is safer.
- Assistant features become more useful.
- The team builds on a working channel, not assumptions.
