# WhatsApp Plugin

Client-facing product promo and feature summary

Prepared: June 24, 2026

## Product Positioning

The WhatsApp plugin is the communication foundation for WhatsApp-led businesses inside WebXpanse and Clarity CRM. It gives a workspace a clear place to connect WhatsApp Business, confirm webhook readiness, register or migrate numbers, send approved messages, manage queued delivery, and track WhatsApp activity inside the CRM.

This plugin is not the WhatsApp Assistant. WhatsApp Assistant is the AI-assisted layer that can send digests, receive internal instructions, and help a team act through WhatsApp after the channel is ready. The WhatsApp plugin comes first. It makes sure the business number, access token, webhook, queue, templates, and delivery tracking are dependable before assistant behavior is added.

For a client, the value is simple: WhatsApp helps turn fast conversations into trackable follow-up.

## Executive Summary

Many small businesses already live on WhatsApp. Leads ask questions there. Customers send photos, voice notes, and quick replies. Owners answer from their phones. Sales teams follow up through chat because it feels faster than email. The problem is not that WhatsApp is popular. The problem is that WhatsApp conversations can become scattered, personal, and hard to track.

The WhatsApp plugin brings that channel into the business system. It helps owners and admins set up the WhatsApp Business connection, save the right phone number details, confirm the webhook route, register or migrate numbers when needed, check readiness, send messages, process queued sends, and see whether messages are pending, sent, delivered, read, or failed.

The result is not "more WhatsApp features." The result is a cleaner communication engine for businesses whose customers already prefer WhatsApp.

## Product At A Glance

- Operating role: Core WhatsApp Business channel setup, webhook readiness, sending, queueing, number management, and WhatsApp activity tracking.
- Primary audience: Entrepreneurs, founders, sales teams, customer support teams, field service teams, account managers, and WhatsApp-first businesses.
- Core promise: Make WhatsApp dependable enough for customer communication, follow-up, and later automation.
- Key motion: Save WhatsApp Business credentials, copy the workspace webhook into Meta, run readiness checks, send approved messages, and track the result inside the CRM.

## What The WhatsApp Plugin Is

The WhatsApp plugin is an installable core communication plugin inside WebXpanse and Clarity CRM.

It owns the WhatsApp Business channel setup for the workspace. It handles the practical foundation that other WhatsApp tools need: phone number identity, access token storage, webhook callback details, verify token, setup readiness, Cloud API number registration, On-Premises migration support, message sending, approved templates, message queueing, delivery tracking, and CRM activity records.

In simple terms, it gives the workspace:

- A place to configure WhatsApp Business.
- A way to confirm whether sending and receiving are ready.
- A workspace webhook for Meta callback setup.
- A place to register or migrate WhatsApp numbers.
- A way to send individual and bulk WhatsApp messages.
- A queue so larger sends can be processed in a controlled way.
- A messages page so the team can see status and failures.
- A foundation that WhatsApp Assistant can safely build on later.

## What The WhatsApp Plugin Is Not

The WhatsApp plugin should be sold clearly.

It is not the WhatsApp Assistant. It does not replace the assistant layer for digests, internal instructions, session coaching, or AI-guided WhatsApp workflows. It does not bypass WhatsApp Business rules. It does not guarantee Meta business approval, template approval, deliverability, phone-number ownership, or customer consent. It does not turn WhatsApp into an uncontrolled chatbot.

It is the foundation.

That is why it matters. A business should make the WhatsApp channel dependable before placing AI, bulk messaging, or automation on top of it.

## Salient Features

### 1. Marketplace Setup

The WhatsApp plugin is managed from the Marketplace as a protected communication plugin. This gives owners and admins a visible place to understand whether the WhatsApp channel is installed, configured, and ready.

Client value:

- Clear onboarding.
- Less confusion about where WhatsApp setup lives.
- A stronger foundation before customer messages are sent.

### 2. Required Core Channel

The plugin is treated as a required core channel for WhatsApp-led workspaces. It is protected because other WhatsApp features depend on it.

Client value:

- WhatsApp becomes business infrastructure, not a side feature.
- Assistant and automation tools can wait until the channel is ready.
- Admins can see the base channel before layering on advanced behavior.

### 3. Manual Cloud API Setup

Admins can save the WhatsApp phone number ID, display number, access token, verified name, WhatsApp Business Account ID, Meta business ID, token expiry, and setup notes.

Client value:

- The business knows which WhatsApp number is connected.
- Credentials are handled through a clear setup path.
- The workspace can check whether outbound sending is ready.

### 4. Business Number Identity

The setup tracks the display phone number and verified name. This helps the team understand the sender identity customers will recognize.

Client value:

- Customers see a consistent WhatsApp identity.
- Admins can avoid connecting the wrong number.
- The team can match the channel to the correct business brand.

### 5. WhatsApp Business Account Details

The plugin stores WhatsApp Business Account and Meta business details when available. This keeps the channel tied to the correct business assets.

Client value:

- Easier administration.
- Better visibility into which Meta assets support the channel.
- Less guesswork during setup or troubleshooting.

### 6. Access Token Awareness

The plugin tracks whether an access token is saved and can record token expiry. This matters because sending depends on valid WhatsApp Business credentials.

Client value:

- Admins can see whether credentials are present.
- Expiring or missing tokens are easier to investigate.
- Failed sending is easier to explain.

### 7. Workspace Webhook

The WhatsApp setup includes a workspace webhook callback URL. Admins copy this URL into Meta so inbound messages and delivery callbacks can reach the CRM.

Client value:

- Inbound WhatsApp events can be routed into the workspace.
- Setup becomes a guided process instead of a mystery.
- The business can prepare for two-way WhatsApp communication.

### 8. Verify Token Support

The setup provides a webhook verify token for Meta verification. Admins can copy the token from the plugin and use it during Meta webhook configuration.

Client value:

- Webhook setup is easier to complete.
- Admins have a clear value to copy into Meta.
- The system can show whether verification has happened.

### 9. Webhook Readiness Checks

The plugin checks whether the workspace URL, verify token, and Meta verification are ready. It also shows the last webhook event when available.

Client value:

- Admins can see what is missing.
- Inbound setup problems are easier to diagnose.
- The team avoids relying on WhatsApp before callbacks are ready.

### 10. Inbound Message Capture

When webhook setup is ready, inbound WhatsApp messages can be captured and connected to CRM communication records.

Client value:

- Customer replies can become part of the business record.
- Conversations are easier to review later.
- WhatsApp activity does not have to stay hidden on one phone.

### 11. Delivery Status Updates

The webhook can update message status as WhatsApp events arrive. Messages can move through statuses such as sent, delivered, read, or failed.

Client value:

- The team can see whether a message actually moved.
- Failed messages become visible instead of silent.
- Follow-up decisions can be based on real status.

### 12. Channel Health Labels

The readiness system can show states such as WhatsApp not connected, WhatsApp number connected, sending ready, webhook needs setup, or setup needs attention.

Client value:

- Admins get plain-language status.
- Setup gaps are easier to understand.
- The business knows what to fix next.

### 13. Sending Readiness

The plugin checks whether the phone number ID and access token are present. These are key pieces for outbound WhatsApp sending.

Client value:

- The team can confirm sending readiness before using WhatsApp.
- Missing credentials are easier to spot.
- Real customer communication starts from a tested foundation.

### 14. Inbound Readiness

The plugin checks whether webhook details and verification are in place for receiving messages and status callbacks.

Client value:

- The team knows whether WhatsApp can support two-way visibility.
- Inbound setup is not treated as an afterthought.
- Assistant and automation layers get a safer foundation.

### 15. Optional Meta Embedded Signup

When platform configuration is available, the plugin can show Meta embedded signup as an optional setup shortcut.

Client value:

- The business can use a guided Meta flow when supported.
- Manual setup remains available when embedded signup is not configured.
- The client is not blocked by one setup method.

### 16. Number Registration

The plugin includes a registration path for Cloud API numbers, including fields such as PIN, backup password, region, and metadata.

Client value:

- Number readiness can be handled inside the workspace setup area.
- Admins have a guided path for Cloud API registration.
- The business can move from setup to operational use more confidently.

### 17. On-Premises Migration Support

The plugin can support On-Premises to Cloud API migration metadata when the required On-Premises configuration is available.

Client value:

- Businesses moving from older WhatsApp setups have a clearer transition path.
- Migration work is visible instead of scattered across tools.
- The workspace can support future-ready WhatsApp infrastructure.

### 18. Advanced Deregistration Control

The migration area includes advanced deregistration controls with confirmation requirements.

Client value:

- Risky channel actions are separated from normal setup.
- Admins must confirm before disconnecting a number.
- The business has safer controls around important WhatsApp assets.

### 19. Individual WhatsApp Sending

The workspace can send direct WhatsApp messages when the business has a valid session and setup is ready.

Client value:

- Users can follow up from CRM context.
- WhatsApp communication can stay tied to customer records.
- Fast replies become part of the business workflow.

### 20. Approved Template Messaging

The plugin supports approved WhatsApp template messages for business-initiated communication. Templates can include body, header, button, and carousel-style parameters when supported by the approved template structure.

Client value:

- The business can start conversations using approved templates.
- Repeated messages become more consistent.
- WhatsApp rules are respected instead of ignored.

### 21. Template Browser

Bulk WhatsApp workflows can load approved templates from the connected WhatsApp Business account.

Client value:

- Users can pick from approved message options.
- Teams avoid guessing template names.
- Campaign setup becomes easier for non-technical users.

### 22. Template Parameter Validation

The sending logic checks template parameters against expected structures. This helps catch missing or mismatched fields before messages fail.

Client value:

- Fewer broken sends.
- Better guidance when templates need the right values.
- More confidence when using structured WhatsApp templates.

### 23. WhatsApp Session Awareness

The plugin respects WhatsApp's customer-service window. Free-form text messages are intended for active conversations, while approved templates are used when the business starts or reopens a conversation.

Client value:

- The system encourages proper WhatsApp usage.
- Users understand why a template may be required.
- The business avoids building habits around messages that cannot be sent.

### 24. Bulk WhatsApp

The plugin supports bulk WhatsApp workflows where recipients can be selected and messages can be queued.

Client value:

- The business can send reminders, announcements, or follow-ups to selected groups.
- Bulk activity is more controlled than manual copy-and-paste messaging.
- Teams can use templates for repeatable communication.

### 25. Queueing

Messages can be placed into a WhatsApp queue. The queue supports pending, processing, completed, failed, and retry behavior.

Client value:

- The business avoids forcing every send to happen instantly.
- Larger sends can be processed in a more controlled way.
- Failed or pending work is easier to review.

### 26. Send Pending Messages

The WhatsApp messages area includes an operational path to process pending messages. This means teams can send queued messages without needing a technical command line.

Client value:

- Non-technical users can move queued messages forward.
- Admins can control when a batch is processed.
- The business can review before delivery.

### 27. Background Worker Option

For businesses that want more automation, a WhatsApp worker can process queued messages continuously in the background.

Client value:

- Teams can start manually and automate later.
- Growing operations can scale message processing.
- The system supports both simple and advanced operating styles.

### 28. Retry And Rate Limit Handling

Queue processing can retry certain temporary failures, including rate-limit style failures, after a delay.

Client value:

- Temporary problems do not automatically become permanent failures.
- The system gives sends a safer second chance.
- Admins get a clearer picture of what failed and why.

### 29. Queue Clearing And Cancellation

Pending queued messages can be cleared when the business decides not to send them.

Client value:

- Teams can stop a batch before it goes out.
- Admins are not trapped by old pending sends.
- The business keeps control over customer communication.

### 30. WhatsApp Messages Page

The WhatsApp messages area gives the team a place to review sent, pending, delivered, read, and failed messages.

Client value:

- The team can see what happened.
- Follow-up becomes easier to manage.
- Failed communication can be noticed and corrected.

### 31. CRM Communication Records

WhatsApp messages can be connected to CRM communication history. Inbound and outbound activity can become part of the contact story.

Client value:

- Customer history becomes easier to review.
- Teams can understand what was said before calling or following up.
- WhatsApp becomes part of the CRM instead of a separate memory.

### 32. Contact Matching

Inbound WhatsApp activity can be matched to contacts using phone numbers. This helps connect chat activity to the right customer record.

Client value:

- Conversations are easier to attach to the right person.
- Teams can avoid losing context.
- Customer records become more complete.

### 33. WhatsApp Assistant Foundation

The WhatsApp plugin prepares the ground for WhatsApp Assistant by making the base channel ready first.

Client value:

- AI workflows start from a working communication channel.
