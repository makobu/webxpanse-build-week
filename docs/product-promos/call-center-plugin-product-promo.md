# Voice & Call Center Plugin Product Promo

## Product Positioning

The Voice & Call Center plugin turns business phone calls into a managed customer workflow.

The client is not buying a phone screen. The client is buying a safer way to answer customers, route calls, remember what was said, and turn conversations into follow-up.

For a growing business, calls are often where the real decision happens. A lead asks a question. A customer explains a problem. A prospect gives an objection. A buyer promises to send information. If that call is not captured, the business loses memory. If the next step is not clear, the team loses momentum.

Voice & Call Center helps solve that problem by connecting call activity to the customer record. It gives agents a place to manage availability, queue calls, review recent conversations, link unknown callers, and use approved AI summaries without giving up human control.

## Executive Summary

The Voice & Call Center plugin is an AI-first calling and call operations module for Clarity CRM. It supports secure inbound and outbound business calling, call queues, agent endpoints, consented recording, post-call transcription, evidence-backed call summaries, and Customer Voice insights.

The page has two practical roles:

1. The Call Center page gives agents a working area for voice operations.
2. The Marketplace setup page gives owners and administrators the control panel for provider setup, routing, consent, retention, testing, usage, and AI automation policy.

Together, those two areas help a business move from scattered phone calls to organized customer conversations.

The result is simple: fewer missed opportunities, cleaner follow-up, better customer memory, and stronger visibility into what customers are really saying.

## Plain English Pitch

Voice & Call Center helps your business stop losing important details from phone calls.

Instead of treating calls as separate from the CRM, it connects them to contacts, activities, communications, tasks, and customer insight. Your team can see who called, who handled it, whether the call was missed, what the next step is, and whether the call created useful follow-up.

When recording, consent, transcription, and AI review are enabled, the system can help turn a call into a summary, a next action, a task suggestion, or a recurring Customer Voice theme. ## What The Call Center Page Is About

The Call Center page is the live operations screen for voice work.

It is built for the person who needs to place calls, receive assignments, check availability, monitor active sessions, review call history, and open call intelligence when permission allows.

The page is not meant to feel like a complicated telecom console. It is meant to be a practical CRM work area:

- Set availability.
- Dial a customer or phone number.
- See live call assignments.
- Watch queue health.
- Review recent calls.
- Filter missed or unmatched calls.
- Link unknown callers to contacts.
- Set call outcomes.
- Review AI-generated call context.
- Export or delete protected call evidence when authorized.

The page keeps the call tied to business work. A call is not just an event. It becomes part of the customer journey.

## What The Marketplace Setup Page Is About

The Marketplace setup page is the owner and administrator control panel for the plugin.

It helps the business prepare voice operations before live use. It is where the workspace connects provider credentials, adds agents, sets queues, configures business hours, chooses fallback routing, controls consent, sets retention, enables transcription, and runs controlled tests.

This setup area is important because voice is sensitive. A business must decide who can call, what numbers are allowed, how calls are routed, when recording is allowed, how long evidence is kept, and what AI can do with call information.

The setup page is built to make those decisions visible before the team starts relying on voice.

## Salient Features

### 1. Agent Availability

Agents can set their status as available, away, or offline.

This helps the system understand who is ready to handle voice work. It also helps managers avoid routing customers to someone who is not ready.

Client result: calls are handled by available people, not lost in confusion.

### 2. Verified Agent Endpoints

Agents use configured phone numbers or SIP endpoints.

This means the plugin works with real agent endpoints instead of pretending every call happens inside the browser. The system can ring the verified agent endpoint first, then connect the call through the provider.

Client result: the business can use controlled voice operations while keeping provider media outside the CRM application.

### 3. Outbound Dialing From CRM Contacts

The Call Center page lets an agent choose a contact or enter a telephone number.

When a contact is selected, the system checks that the destination matches the contact phone number. Manual destinations require confirmation. This reduces careless dialing and protects the customer record.

Client result: agents can move quickly while the system still checks the basics.

### 4. Policy-Checked Calling

The plugin checks destination rules before placing calls.

Kenya destinations are allowed by default, premium and special-rate prefixes are blocked, and international calling requires owner configuration. Workspaces can also set blocked prefixes and call limits.

Client result: the business gets control over calling risk and cost exposure.

### 5. Queue Health

The Call Center page shows queue status, waiting calls, active calls, open or closed state, and agent availability.

This gives the team a quick view of routing pressure. Managers can see when a queue is healthy and when customer demand is building up.

Client result: the team can react before customers feel ignored.

### 6. Live Assignments

The live operations panel shows active call assignments.

It can show the call state, agent name, direction, and available controls. Where provider capability allows, the system can support actions such as transfer or ending a call. Where the provider does not support remote ending, agents hang up from their phone or SIP client.

Client result: live calls become visible work, not hidden activity.

### 7. Recent Call History

The page includes a recent call history table.

It shows the call, state, agent, AI status, context options, duration, and time. Users can filter all calls, missed calls, and unmatched calls.

Client result: managers and agents can quickly find calls that need attention.

### 8. Missed And Unmatched Call Review

The page makes it easy to identify missed and unmatched calls.

Unmatched callers stay unmatched until an authorized user links them to an existing contact or creates a new contact. This avoids guessing and protects CRM data quality.

Client result: the business can recover opportunities without polluting the customer database.

### 9. Contact Linking

Unknown callers can be linked to existing contacts or turned into new contacts.

This is useful when a customer calls from a number that was not already in the CRM. The system gives the team a deliberate matching step.

Client result: the business can turn anonymous call activity into useful customer context.

### 10. Call Outcomes

Agents can set outcomes such as connected, follow up, qualified, not interested, wrong number, voicemail, or other.

This helps the CRM reflect what happened after the call instead of simply recording that a call occurred.

Client result: follow-up becomes easier because the outcome is visible.

### 11. Consented Recording

Recording is controlled by consent settings and workspace-owner acknowledgement.

The plugin does not treat recording as a casual switch. Recording requires consent configuration, a notice or consent flow, retention settings, package entitlement, and current owner acknowledgement.

Client result: the business can use recording responsibly, with clear ownership of policy decisions.

### 12. Post-Call Transcription

When recording, consent, entitlement, worker health, and the workspace AI key are ready, the system can process call recordings into transcripts.

The transcription model is configured in setup, and the system uses the OpenAI key already saved in workspace Settings. It does not create a second AI-key store for this plugin.

Client result: important calls can become searchable, reviewable text instead of disappearing after they end.

### 13. AI Call Intelligence

The plugin can analyze a call transcript and produce structured insight.

That insight can include:

- Summary.
- Relationship context.
- Sentiment.
- Intent.
- Pain points.
- Goals.
- Objections.
- Commitments.
- Requested actions.
- Next step.
- Task suggestions.
- Contact update suggestions.
- Deal-stage suggestions.

Client result: the team gets a practical after-call brief without forcing an agent to remember every detail manually.

### 14. Reviewable CRM Actions

AI suggestions are controlled by workspace automation policy.

The system can create approved summaries and, when policy allows, add contact context or follow-up tasks. Sensitive actions stay limited. Production v1 does not automatically move deals, send follow-up messages, replace contact fields, run unattended outbound calling, or publish campaigns.

Client result: AI helps prepare work, but people stay in charge.

### 15. Protected Evidence Access

Authorized users can review transcripts, listen to recordings, export evidence, or delete CRM-held raw transcript content and recording pointers.

Recording URLs remain server-side. Transcript access is permission-controlled and workspace-scoped.

Client result: call evidence is useful, but not casually exposed.

### 16. Customer Voice Insights

Customer Voice turns repeated call themes into reviewable marketing context.

It only surfaces recurring themes after at least three distinct contacts support them. Raw transcripts and direct identifiers are not shown on the Customer Voice page. Accepted themes become draft marketing context and still require normal review.

Client result: the business can learn what customers keep asking for, complaining about, or trying to achieve.

### 17. Setup Readiness Checks

The setup area checks whether the workspace is ready for voice.

It looks at platform enablement, package entitlement, plugin status, provider credentials, virtual number, callback URL, verified provider status, agent endpoints, queue routing, recording entitlement, transcription entitlement, Customer Voice entitlement, consent, owner acknowledgement, workspace AI key, transcription model, and worker health.

Client result: the business can see what is missing before live call operations begin.

### 18. Queues, Hours, And Fallback

Administrators can configure named queues, open days, open times, timezone, maximum wait, default route, queue members, and fallback behavior.

Fallback options include rejecting politely, sending to a verified number, or routing to an alternate queue.

Client result: customers can be routed according to the business's real working hours and staffing.

### 19. Usage And Capacity Controls

The plugin respects package limits and workspace limits.

It tracks active calls, concurrency, hourly call limits, daily minute limits, provider usage estimates, and worker health. Growth Studio Voice and Scale Voice packages can include voice agent and concurrent call limits.

Client result: owners can manage voice activity without opening the door to uncontrolled usage.

### 20. Provider Boundary

The CRM controls call state, routing, callbacks, policy, usage estimates, and post-call intelligence. The provider carries the audio.

This boundary matters. It keeps long-running audio media outside the PHP app while still bringing the useful call context back into the CRM.

Client result: the business gets CRM-connected voice without pretending the CRM is the telephone carrier.

## Product Intentions

### Intention 1: Make Calls Part Of The Customer Record

The plugin is designed so completed matched calls can become CRM activities and voice communications.

That gives the business a shared record of what happened, who handled it, and what should happen next.

### Intention 2: Reduce Missed Follow-Up

Many phone conversations create promises:

- Send a quote.
- Call back tomorrow.
- Book a demo.
- Update account details.
- Escalate a problem.
- Share pricing.
- Confirm availability.

Voice & Call Center helps turn those promises into visible next steps.

### Intention 3: Keep Humans In Control

The plugin uses AI carefully.

It can summarize, suggest, and prepare. It does not assume that every AI idea should become an action. Review, approval, permissions, and policy remain central.

### Intention 4: Protect Sensitive Information

Voice data can be sensitive. The plugin includes consent controls, retention settings, permission checks, server-side recording access, encrypted transcripts, explicit evidence export, and deletion controls for CRM-held raw evidence.

The intention is not to collect everything forever. The intention is to make useful call evidence available to the right people under clear rules.

### Intention 5: Give Managers Operational Visibility

Managers need to know whether calls are being answered, who is available, whether queues are healthy, and which calls need review.

The page turns voice work into visible operations.

### Intention 6: Turn Repeated Customer Feedback Into Strategy

One call can be useful. Many calls can reveal a pattern.

Customer Voice helps identify repeated pains, goals, objections, requested features, and buying language after enough distinct contacts support the theme.

The intention is to help the business learn from customers at scale without exposing private customer details.
