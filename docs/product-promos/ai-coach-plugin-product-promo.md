# AI Coach Plugin

Client-facing product promo and feature summary

Prepared: June 21, 2026

## Product Positioning

The AI Coach plugin is the proactive "what should I do next?" layer for WebXpanse and Clarity CRM. It helps an entrepreneur start the day with clearer priorities, better follow-up, and a practical next move based on what is already happening in the business.

AI Coach is not a generic chatbot. It does not sit outside the business and guess. It reads the workspace context, checks readiness, looks at CRM activity, respects the Clarity Journey foundation, and then recommends useful actions the owner can review and turn into tasks.

For a client, the value is simple: the system helps the business move from scattered CRM data to focused daily action.

## Executive Summary

AI Coach gives small teams a decision-ready guidance layer inside the dashboard. It helps the owner understand what needs attention now, what setup is missing, which follow-ups are slipping, where strategy context is weak, and what quick wins are available.

The plugin is designed for entrepreneurs who do not want vague AI advice. They want a partner that understands the current workspace, the company profile, the product or offer, the completed Clarity Journey, active CRM signals, readiness gaps, tasks, deals, contacts, targets, and available skills.

AI Coach turns that context into recommendation cards. Each card can explain why it appeared, how much trust the system has in the recommendation, what evidence was used, and what the user can do next. When the recommendation should become work, the user can create a task with subtasks and source context attached.

The result is not "AI for the sake of AI." The result is a calmer operating rhythm where the owner knows what deserves attention and can move forward with confidence.

AI Coach solves this by putting readiness before advice.

## What AI Coach Is

AI Coach is an installable AI skill inside WebXpanse and Clarity CRM.

It acts as a dashboard coaching layer. It reads available context, checks readiness, builds recommendation cards, and helps the user create tasks from the best next steps.

The plugin can use:

- Installed skill contracts.
- Company profile and product or offer context.
- Completed Clarity Journey data.
- Optional personal strategy refinements.
- CRM contacts, deals, tasks, targets, and activity.
- Channel and Marketplace setup status.
- User role and work context.
- Feedback from previous recommendations.
- Finance and operating evidence where available.

AI Coach is designed to guide action without pretending to own every business decision. It works best when the business has completed the right setup and when related advice skills are ready.

## Salient Features

### Proactive Dashboard Coaching

AI Coach is built into the daily dashboard experience. The user does not have to start with a blank chat prompt or figure out what to ask.

The coach brings recommendations forward in sections such as foundation gaps, today's priorities, quick wins, and missing features. This makes the CRM feel less like a storage system and more like a working partner.

The result: the owner can begin with direction instead of digging through screens.

### Readiness-First Guidance

AI Coach checks whether the workspace is ready before giving stronger business recommendations.

It can look for key readiness items such as AI Coach being installed and enabled, company context, product or offer context, completed Clarity Journey, AI services, optional personal strategy details, and ready advice skills.

If important context is missing, AI Coach does not fake confidence. It nudges the user toward the missing setup first.

The result: the system protects the client from weak advice built on weak inputs.

### Clarity Journey Source Of Truth

AI Coach treats Clarity Journey as the main strategy foundation. It uses the journey to understand the customer, problem, offer, go-to-market plan, MVP test, growth metrics, and execution goals.

Before Clarity Journey is complete, AI Coach can point the owner back to the next missing stage. After it is complete, AI Coach can give better recommendations because it is grounded in the business model and strategy context.

The result: AI advice becomes more specific, more disciplined, and less generic.

### Clear Recommendation Sections

AI Coach organizes recommendations into practical groups:

- Foundation gaps: setup or strategy items that need attention before better guidance is possible.
- Today's priorities: the highest impact actions the user should consider now.
- Quick wins: useful actions that are easier to complete.
- Missing features: Marketplace or system capabilities that may help the business move forward.

This structure helps the user separate urgent work from setup work, and simple wins from bigger operating gaps.

The result: the owner can scan recommendations quickly and choose a sensible next move.

### Why This Appeared

Each recommendation can show why it appeared. For example, the system may connect a card to missing setup, stale deal activity, incomplete strategy context, low outreach activity, or a ready Marketplace pairing.

This matters because users trust guidance more when they can see the reason behind it.

The result: recommendations feel explainable instead of mysterious.

### Trust Signals

AI Coach can show trust signals such as context quality, confidence, source, goal match, readiness status, and whether the recommendation came from live AI, cached guidance, fallback logic, or setup rules.

This helps the user understand how much weight to put on the card.

The result: the client gets AI guidance with visible guardrails, not blind confidence.

### Feedback-Aware Learning

AI Coach learns conservatively from explicit feedback. Users can mark recommendations as useful, not relevant, already done, or ask to see fewer similar suggestions.

The system can use that feedback to boost useful patterns, downrank weak patterns, explain future cards better, or suppress repeated low-value recommendations.

The result: AI Coach improves with the business instead of repeating the same advice forever.

### Follow-Through Task Creation

A recommendation becomes more valuable when it can become real work.

AI Coach can create task candidates with suggested subtasks. The task can preserve the recommendation source, guidance run, target record, evidence, trust signals, and related metadata.

This makes it easier to move from "good idea" to "assigned work."

The result: advice does not get lost. It becomes trackable action.

### Operating Maturity Guidance

AI Coach understands that every business is not in the same stage.

A founder who has not finished Clarity Journey needs different guidance from a team already running weekly operating commitments. The coach can adjust its next steps based on operating maturity, such as:

- Complete the next Clarity Journey stage.
- Start Founder Loop first-deal commitments.
- Work the current weekly commitment.
- Compare real operating evidence against strategy assumptions.

The result: AI Coach recommends the next move for the stage the business is actually in.

### Goal-Aware And Role-Aware Priorities

AI Coach can consider user role, work context, active targets, and business goals when ranking recommendations.

A founder, sales manager, operator, or admin may need different priorities. The system can score relevance and avoid pushing low-fit recommendations when the available context does not support them.

The result: recommendations feel closer to the user's real job.

### Assumption Conflict Detection

AI Coach can help spot gaps between what the business planned and what the evidence shows.

For example, it may notice that the target customer in the strategy does not match the contacts being worked, that the go-to-market channel is not showing up in lead sources, or that spending evidence does not match the planned validation stage.

The result: the owner can catch strategy drift before it becomes wasted time or money.

### Finance And Evidence Guardrails

AI Coach can use finance and operating evidence to keep recommendations practical.

Before recommending spend, growth pushes, or advanced campaigns, it can consider money-related context such as runway, budget, cost, margin, break-even, receivables, payables, and report quality where available.

The result: the system is more likely to recommend sensible next steps for the business's real condition.

### Marketplace Setup Nudges

AI Coach can recommend Marketplace setup when a missing skill or incomplete module is blocking better follow-through.

For example, if the business wants stronger marketing support, AI Coach may point toward Campaign Manager. If strategy context is weak, it may point back to Clarity Journey or Lean Canvas compatibility. If communication follow-through is needed, it may suggest a channel module such as email, WhatsApp, calendar, or meeting support.

The result: the client sees which capability to add next and why it matters.

### Personal Strategy Lens

AI Coach can include optional personal strategy refinements. A user can add details such as target market focus, market view, strategy hypothesis, tone preference, and personal voice notes.

This helps the same business system serve different people with different operating styles while still keeping the shared business foundation intact.

The result: guidance can feel more personal without losing the company source of truth.

### Smart Templates

AI Coach connects with Smart Templates readiness and generation flows for email or workflow template packs when the workspace is ready.

This gives the system a path from recommendation to useful business materials.

The result: the owner can move from "we should follow up better" to reusable templates and practical execution support.

### Coaching Modes And Runtime Controls

AI Coach supports different coaching modes, including foundation-focused guidance, operations-focused guidance, and guardian-style monitoring.

The workspace can also use runtime controls such as paused or diagnostics-only behavior. When the coach is paused or limited, it should explain the state instead of pretending to operate normally.

The result: the business can control when AI is active, limited, or used for diagnosis only.

### Diagnostics And Admin Tuning

AI Coach can provide diagnostics about recommendation quality, confidence, context gaps, suppressed cards, mode, readiness blockers, feedback patterns, and operating maturity.

Admins can review patterns and tune recommendation behavior when needed, such as boosting useful categories, muting noisy patterns, or resetting learning.

The result: AI guidance becomes manageable as a system, not just a black box.

## What AI Coach Does Not Do

AI Coach is intentionally bounded.

It does not replace the Clarity Journey or own the business model fields. It does not unlock generic strategy advice when the right advice skill is not ready. It does not run email, WhatsApp, SMS, calendar, or workflow automation by itself. It does not take control away from the user.

Instead, it recommends, explains, nudges, and helps create tasks. The owner still reviews and decides.

The result: clients get helpful AI without losing human control.

## Product Intentions

AI Coach is built around five clear intentions.

First, it helps the user know the next move. The coach should make the dashboard feel useful at the start of the day.

Second, it protects the quality of AI advice. The system should check readiness before giving recommendations that depend on business context.

Third, it connects strategy to execution. Clarity Journey, CRM activity, tasks, contacts, deals, and targets should not live in separate worlds.

Fourth, it keeps humans in control. AI Coach recommends and explains; the user reviews, gives feedback, and decides.

Fifth, it helps the system learn slowly and safely. Feedback should improve future recommendations without letting the AI overreact to one click.
