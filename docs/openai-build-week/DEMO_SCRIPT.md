# Three-minute demo script

## Recording setup

- Use the `Solo Founder Operating Rhythm` presentation seed pack.
- Start on the dashboard at desktop width with the browser zoom at 100%.
- Confirm the top card shows: `Business context · 100%`, `3 active pipeline signals`, and `Send first 20 warm outreach messages`.
- Close unrelated notifications and do not scroll below the founder operating-rhythm card until the final proof shot.
- Record a clean voiceover. Aim for 2:35–2:50 so the final upload remains safely under three minutes.

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem

On screen: the founder command center.

> I am Dennis Makobu, founder and solo builder of WebXpanse. Solo founders do not need another dashboard full of numbers. They need to know what matters now, why it matters, and what action will create evidence.

## 0:25–0:55 — Business context

Open `Business context · 100%` and briefly show the completed dimensions.

> Clarity begins with structured business context: the company, customer, offer, strategy, Lean Canvas, and founder journey. It combines that with live CRM signals instead of asking a general-purpose model to guess what business I run.

Close the panel.

## 0:55–1:25 — Ranked constraint

Point to `Convert active pipeline into paid proof`, `3 active pipeline signals`, and the founder-work lane.

> In this workspace the context is complete, three opportunities are active, and none has become paid proof. Clarity ranks that as the current constraint. It separates work requiring founder judgment from work the system has actually completed, so it does not pretend automation happened.

## 1:25–2:05 — Ask Clarity why

Click `Ask Clarity why`. Let the prefilled evidence question appear and wait for the complete answer.

> The handoff includes the exact constraint and evidence visible on the dashboard. The OpenAI model explains why this is the priority, what remains a founder decision, and the next measurable action. The response is grounded in three open deals, zero wins, and the proposal-stage opportunity—not a generic CRM answer.

Keep the final answer visible long enough to read its first two sentences.

## 2:05–2:35 — From decision to action

Close Clarity and click the `Send first 20 warm outreach messages` link in the current-commitment footer. Show the task title, due date, `You (demo owner)`, and the review-first Clarity completion controls.

> Advice only matters when it becomes execution. The chosen move is connected to a dated commitment and a real CRM task. Replies, deal movement, and paid proof feed the next review, closing the learning loop.

## 2:35–2:55 — Impact and close

Return to the command center.

> WebXpanse turns a CRM into a calm operating rhythm for one-person companies: context, constraint, explanation, action, and measurable evidence. I used Codex with GPT-5.6 to design and implement the command center, trace bugs, write focused tests, and verify this browser flow. The working product uses the OpenAI Responses API for Clarity's contextual explanation. This is Clarity—an AI co-founder that keeps the founder in control.

End on the full founder command-center card and the WebXpanse/Clarity branding.

## Recording acceptance check

- The recording is three minutes or shorter.
- Text is readable at normal playback speed.
- No passwords, API keys, private email, local database tools, or browser notifications appear.
- The Clarity answer finishes before moving on.
- The video shows a working product, not slides alone.
- Upload to YouTube as public, then verify the link in a logged-out window.
