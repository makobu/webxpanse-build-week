# Positioning And Wedge

## Core Positioning

Do not position this company as:

- another CRM
- a cheaper Salesforce
- an all-in-one SMB suite

Position it as:

`AI operating CRM for distributors and wholesalers in WhatsApp-first markets`

Short version:

`We help distributors and wholesalers run inbound sales, quoting, follow-up, and collections from a controlled AI-driven operating layer, with CRM as the system of record and system of action.`

## Why This Wedge Fits The Product

The current product already supports the core pieces of this story:

- unified inbox with email and WhatsApp support
- deals and stage progression
- quotes, invoices, and commercial automation
- tasks and follow-through workflows
- reporting and auditability
- AI control, autonomy gating, and takeover-confidence thinking

This means the pitch can honestly say:

- the CRM stores the context
- the AI handles the operational work
- the governance layer controls risk

## ICP

Primary ICP:

- distributors and wholesalers
- 5-100 sales and ops staff
- teams that already sell over WhatsApp, email, and phone
- environments where response speed, quote turnaround, and follow-up discipline directly affect revenue

Best geographies to test first:

- Africa
- Middle East
- other WhatsApp-heavy business environments

## First Painful Workflow

The opening wedge is:

`inbound inquiry -> qualification -> quote/proforma draft -> follow-up -> deal progression -> invoice/collection follow-through`

This wedge is strong because:

- it is high frequency
- it is painful
- it touches revenue directly
- incumbents are usually weak at message-first execution
- AI can visibly save time and recover lost deals

## What The Product Is Not Yet Claiming

Do not claim:

- full business takeover today
- full horizontal CRM replacement
- fully autonomous customer communication without guardrails
- enterprise-grade parity with incumbents across every module

Do claim:

- control-heavy AI operations for a narrow workflow
- secure and auditable execution
- progression from copilot to governed autonomy
- expansion from one workflow wedge into a broader operating system

## Competitive Story

Use this framing:

- incumbents are broad systems of record
- this product is becoming a system of action
- security, auditability, and workflow control are not side features; they are part of the buying reason

Better line:

`HubSpot and Salesforce help teams record work. We are building the layer that helps teams get the work done in message-heavy sales environments.`

Avoid line:

`We are a better CRM than HubSpot.`

## Expansion Path

Expansion after wedge proof:

1. quote and invoice revision automation
2. collections and overdue follow-up
3. task and deal follow-through orchestration
4. broader workflow-generated execution
5. wider system-of-action coverage across the business

## Success Criteria For This Positioning

This positioning is working if:

- prospects immediately understand who it is for
- design partners say they would buy the wedge even if they keep their current CRM
- investors repeat the wedge back clearly after one meeting
- the story sounds narrower, more painful, and more fundable than `AI CRM`
