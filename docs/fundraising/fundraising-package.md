# Fundraising Package

This folder turns the high-level funding strategy into practical assets you can use immediately for investor outreach and design-partner conversations.

## Chosen Wedge

The package assumes this positioning:

`AI operating CRM for distributors and wholesalers in WhatsApp-first markets`

Why this wedge:

- the product already has unified inbox, WhatsApp, deals, tasks, reporting, workflow automation, quotes/invoices, and AI/autonomy controls
- distributors and wholesalers often run high-friction, message-heavy sales workflows that generic CRMs handle poorly
- secure workspace control, auditability, and workflow governance matter more in these environments than they do for generic SMB software buyers
- this story is easier to fund than a broad `replace HubSpot/Salesforce` pitch

## Working Assumptions

- stage is pre-revenue or very early revenue
- best first capital source is angels, then selected micro-VCs
- first goal is 3-5 design partners, not a large institutional process
- investors should see this as a workflow wedge with expansion, not a generic CRM

## Market Context

- AI continues to absorb outsized venture dollars, but investors are more selective than the 2021 market
- NVCA/PitchBook reported AI/ML captured 65.6% of US VC deal value in 2025
- Crunchbase reported 2025 funding strength overall, while seed remained concentrated and competitive
- Bessemer and Sequoia continue emphasizing vertical AI, systems of action, and narrow high-ROI wedges
