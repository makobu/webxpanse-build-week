# Investor One-Pager

## Company

Working company description:

`An AI operating CRM for distributors and wholesalers in WhatsApp-first markets.`

## Problem

Many distributors and wholesalers run revenue-critical workflows across WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, and disconnected CRMs.

The result:

- slow response times
- inconsistent quoting
- weak follow-up discipline
- deal leakage between inbox, sales, and finance
- poor auditability when teams try to automate

Generic CRMs store data, but they are not designed to run high-frequency message-led commercial operations with strong control and self-hosting requirements.

## Solution

We are building a self-hosted AI operating CRM that turns the CRM from a system of record into a system of action.

The first workflow wedge is:

`inbound inquiry -> qualification -> quote/proforma draft -> follow-up -> deal progression -> invoice/collection follow-through`

The product combines:

- unified inbox
- WhatsApp and email workflows
- deal and task orchestration
- quote and invoice support
- governed AI/autonomy controls
- auditability and safety-gate thinking

## Why Now

- AI is making workflow automation and agent-like execution practical
- investors are actively backing vertical AI and systems of action
- WhatsApp-first commercial teams remain underserved by legacy CRM products
- self-hosted control is increasingly valuable where data sensitivity, customization, or vendor lock-in concerns are high

External context:

- NVCA/PitchBook reported AI/ML captured 65.6% of US VC deal value in 2025
- Crunchbase reported strong 2025 venture activity, while seed remained concentrated and selective
- Bessemer and Sequoia continue emphasizing vertical AI and narrow high-ROI wedges

## Why This Can Win

- narrower starting wedge than incumbent CRMs
- stronger fit for message-heavy commercial workflows
- self-hosted and control-heavy from the start
- AI roadmap is operational, governed, and auditable rather than chat-only
- expansion path from workflow wedge to broader operating platform

## Product Proof Points From Current Build

- contact, deal, task, and reporting foundations are already present
- unified inbox and communication workflows already exist
- quote, invoice, and commercial automation capabilities are already present
- AI control and autonomy governance thinking are already built into the roadmap and audit layer

## Go-To-Market

Initial GTM:

- recruit 3-5 design partners
- prove ROI in one wedge workflow
- convert pilots into paid deployments
- expand within the same account from messaging and quoting into collections and workflow execution

## What We Need To Prove Next

- design partner demand for the narrow wedge
- time-to-value inside 30-60 days
- measurable ROI:
  - faster quote turnaround
  - shorter reply times
  - more recovered leads
  - improved follow-up completion
  - reduced manual ops load

## Raise

Recommended raise framing:

- small pre-seed or angel round
- purpose:
  - finish the wedge
  - land 3-5 design partners
  - build measurable proof
  - sharpen GTM before a larger seed process

## Closing Line

`We are not trying to out-feature Salesforce. We are building an AI-driven, self-hosted operating layer for a painful commercial workflow that legacy CRM products do not run well.`
