# Pitch Deck Outline

This is a 9-slide investor deck built around the chosen wedge.

## Slide 1: Title

Title:

`AI Operating CRM For Distributors And Wholesalers In WhatsApp-First Markets`

Subtitle:

`From system of record to system of action for message-heavy commercial workflows`

Founder note:

- mention your funding fluency and why you picked this market instead of a generic CRM battle

## Slide 2: Problem

Headline:

`Revenue-critical workflows still happen in WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, and human memory`

Points:

- inbound inquiries get lost
- quote turnaround is slow
- follow-up is inconsistent
- finance and sales handoff breaks
- generic CRMs record the mess but do not solve the workflow

## Slide 3: Why Incumbents Lose Here

Headline:

`Legacy CRMs are broad systems of record, not controlled systems of action`

Points:

- too horizontal
- weak for WhatsApp-first execution
- poor fit for teams that need controlled, auditable execution
- automation is often brittle or hard to audit

## Slide 4: Product

Headline:

`One controlled operating layer across inbox, deals, tasks, quoting, and follow-through`

Show:

- unified inbox
- deals
- AI control center
- reporting

## Slide 5: Wedge Workflow

Headline:

`Start with one painful revenue workflow`

Workflow:

- inbound inquiry
- qualification
- quote/proforma draft
- follow-up
- deal progression
- invoice and collection follow-through

Key line:

`The CRM is the context layer. AI does the operational work. Governance controls the risk.`

## Slide 6: Why Now

Headline:

`Vertical AI is being funded, but only when the wedge is narrow and ROI is obvious`

Points:

- AI is attracting outsized venture capital
- investors are looking for workflow-specific systems of action
- secure platform operations and data governance are real buyer needs in some markets
- distributors and wholesalers remain underserved by generic CRM vendors

## Slide 7: Moat

Headline:

`Control, workflow fit, and operational data create the moat`

Points:

- secure workspace governance and clear data portability
- workflow-specific execution layer
- auditability and safety gates
- data from real operational usage
- expansion from wedge into broader operating platform

## Slide 8: GTM

Headline:

`Design partners first, then wedge proof, then expansion`

Points:

- 3-5 design partners
- 30-60 day pilot
- prove ROI
- convert to paid customer workspaces
- expand inside accounts

## Slide 9: Raise

Headline:

`Small pre-seed to finish the wedge and prove repeatable demand`

Points:

- design partners
- product completion for the wedge
- measurable proof package
- readiness for the next fundraise

## Demo Flow For Investor Calls

Use this sequence in the live demo:

1. inbox conversation
2. deal context
3. AI control / governance
4. quote or invoice workflow
5. reporting / audit angle

Keep it under 7 minutes.
