# Design Partner Outreach

## Goal

Get 3-5 design partners before trying to run a broad fundraise.

## Ideal Design Partner

- distributor or wholesaler
- already uses WhatsApp and email heavily for commercial workflow
- has at least one owner or operator who feels the pain directly
- can measure response time, quote turnaround, or follow-up leakage
- is willing to give workflow access and feedback

## Design Partner Offer

Offer:

- hands-on setup
- direct founder support
- tailored workflow configuration
- discounted pilot or free pilot for tightly scoped feedback
- influence over roadmap

Ask in return:

- weekly feedback call
- access to current workflow pain points
- willingness to measure before/after ROI
- testimonial or case study if successful

## 30-60 Day Pilot Shape

Week 1:

- workflow mapping
- baseline metrics
- inbox, deals, quoting, and follow-up configuration

Weeks 2-4:

- live usage on the wedge workflow
- rapid iteration
- monitor failure points and edge cases

Weeks 5-8:

- quantify ROI
- decide on paid rollout
- capture testimonial and investor proof points

## Core Metrics To Track

- average first-response time
- quote turnaround time
- follow-up completion rate
- inquiry-to-quote conversion
- quote-to-close conversion
- overdue invoice follow-up rate
- hours saved per week

## Cold Design Partner Email

Subject:

`Quick question about WhatsApp and quote follow-up`

Body:

`Hi [Name],`

`I’m building a self-hosted AI operating CRM for distributors and wholesalers that run a lot of sales over WhatsApp and email.`

`The first workflow we are focused on is inbound inquiry -> quote/proforma draft -> follow-up -> deal progression -> collections follow-through.`

`I’m looking for 3-5 design partners who feel this pain strongly and want direct input into the product.`

`If this is relevant, I’d love to show you a short demo and learn how your team handles this workflow today.`

`Best,`

`[Your Name]`

## Discovery Questions

- how many inquiries come through WhatsApp versus email
- how long does quote turnaround usually take
- where do follow-ups get lost
- who owns the handoff between sales and finance
- what current tools are in the stack
- what would make this worth paying for even if they keep their current CRM

## Best Validation Sentence

The strongest response you can get is:

`We would use this for this workflow even if we keep our current CRM.`
