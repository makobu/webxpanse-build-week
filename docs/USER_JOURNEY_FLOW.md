# User Journey Flow

## 1. System Setup
- Admin configures company profile, products, pricing, invoicing, channels, users, permissions, and automation defaults.
- Admin decides whether Auto Admin manages AI and automation setup.

## 2. Lead Capture
- New leads enter from forms, manual entry, imports, email, WhatsApp, SMS, or API/integration sources.
- The system creates or matches contacts and logs the first communication event.

## 3. Contact Qualification
- Contact record becomes the operational workspace.
- Users add notes, update business details, review data quality, and see AI/context enrichment.
- Contact intelligence surfaces relationship health, buying role, communication risks, and next best actions.

## 4. Communication Handling
- Messages land in the unified inbox across email, WhatsApp, and SMS.
- Inbox triage scores the conversation, assigns owner/task where allowed, and updates conversation state.
- Users or AI drafts respond through the relevant channel.

## 5. Deal Creation and Pipeline Movement
- Qualified opportunities become deals.
- Users move deals through stages or allow automation to suggest or apply stage progression.
- Deal activity updates pipeline health, related tasks, and commercial triggers.

## 6. Task Execution
- Manual tasks and AI-created tasks are generated from workflow, communication, coaching, or operational gaps.
- Tasks break down into measurable subtasks.
- Evidence-aware completion checks whether work is actually done before completion is finalized.

## 7. Commercial Operations
- Deals can generate quotes, proformas, and invoices.
- Users send documents, track views, status changes, approvals, and payments.
- Commercial automation reacts to invoice and deal milestones.

## 8. Targets and Performance Tracking
- Personal, team, and company targets track goals across deals, invoices, tasks, contacts, and communications.
- Targets can be manual, auto-rollup, or hybrid.
- Forecast, pace, blockers, milestones, and next actions help users stay on track.

## 9. AI Guidance and Assistance
- AI Coach recommends priorities and setup actions.
- Clarity uses operating context, work context, contact intelligence, inbox state, and target health to guide the user.
- AI auto-responder, task completion, and diagnostics remain policy-controlled.

## 10. Notifications and Follow-Through
- The system sends assignment alerts, inbox nudges, operational notices, and AI-driven prompts.
- Users act from notifications, inbox, tasks, deals, and contact pages without losing context.

## 11. Reporting and Review
- Dashboards summarize activity, targets, pipeline, communication load, and execution health.
- Admins review diagnostics, automation health, incidents, and readiness states.

## 12. Continuous Optimization
- CRM activity refreshes contact intelligence, conversation intelligence, and target intelligence.
- Admin and AI guidance surfaces identify setup gaps, execution risks, and growth opportunities.
