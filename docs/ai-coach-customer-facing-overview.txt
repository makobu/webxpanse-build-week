AI COACH CUSTOMER-FACING OVERVIEW

AI Coach is the proactive "what should I do next?" layer inside Clarity CRM.

Instead of waiting for a user to search through dashboards, reports, contacts, deals, tasks, and settings, AI Coach turns the workspace's live context into clear next-step recommendations. It helps teams understand what matters now, what setup is blocking better performance, and which practical actions can move revenue, follow-up, and execution forward.

The value is simple: AI Coach helps every user spend less time guessing and more time acting on the highest-value next move.


THE CUSTOMER OUTCOME

AI Coach helps businesses turn CRM data into daily execution.

Most CRMs store information. AI Coach helps users act on it. It reviews the workspace context, installed skills, company profile, product or offer setup, user strategy brief, contacts, deals, tasks, targets, and activity signals, then presents practical guidance in clear recommendation sections.

Customers get:

- Better focus: users know what to work on next instead of scanning multiple modules.
- Stronger follow-through: recommendations can become task candidates with subtasks and source tracking.
- Faster setup: the system highlights missing company, offer, strategy, or Marketplace setup that blocks better AI guidance.
- More consistent execution: sales, marketing, and operations teams receive recommendations based on the same workspace context.
- More trustworthy AI guidance: recommendation cards explain why they appeared and include confidence, context, and source signals.
- Safer strategic advice: AI Coach stays inside the boundaries of installed, ready skills instead of inventing unsupported business advice.


WHAT AI COACH DOES

AI Coach is a dashboard coaching layer that reads the current workspace and recommends the next best actions.

It is designed to answer questions like:

- What should I focus on today?
- Which setup gaps are stopping the CRM from giving better guidance?
- Which contacts, deals, tasks, or targets need attention?
- Which quick wins can improve follow-up, pipeline movement, or adoption?
- Which Marketplace skills should be installed or completed next?
- Is the workspace ready for business-growth recommendations?

AI Coach is not just a chatbot. It is a recommendation orchestrator. It connects CRM evidence with installed skill contracts and presents decision-ready guidance without forcing the user to prompt the system first.


CORE OUTCOMES AI COACH HELPS DELIVER

1. Daily focus

AI Coach gives users a short list of practical priorities so they can start the day with direction. Instead of wondering whether to follow up, add leads, clean up deals, create tasks, set targets, or configure workflows, users see ranked recommendations based on their current workspace activity.

2. Better sales follow-up

AI Coach can surface actions tied to contacts, deals, tasks, and targets. It helps users avoid missed follow-ups, incomplete deal movement, and stale pipeline activity by turning CRM signals into recommended next steps.

3. Cleaner onboarding and setup

AI Coach highlights missing prerequisites such as company context, product or offer information, personal Coach Brief completion, AI service readiness, and advice-skill coverage. This helps customers finish setup in the right order instead of discovering gaps later.

4. More actionable strategy

When strategy context is complete, AI Coach can use fields such as target market, ideal customer profile, offer angle, segment focus, sales motion, deal movement strategy, outreach posture, positioning notes, market view, and strategy hypothesis to make recommendations more relevant.

5. Lean Canvas-backed business guidance

When Lean Canvas context is enabled and complete, AI Coach can ground recommendations in business model assumptions such as problem, customer segments, unique value proposition, solution, channels, revenue streams, cost structure, key metrics, and unfair advantage.

6. Founder and operator discipline

AI Coach supports business fundamentals such as offer definition, pricing logic, sales process structure, segmentation, marketing channels, follow-up discipline, revenue forecasting, KPI tracking, pipeline hygiene, and lead quality.

7. Safer AI adoption

AI Coach includes readiness checks, recommendation boundaries, trust signals, explanation chips, diagnostics, and feedback-aware ranking. This gives teams more confidence in the recommendations they choose to act on.


MAIN FEATURE AREAS

Recommendation sections

AI Coach organizes guidance into practical sections:

- Foundation gaps: missing business or workspace fundamentals that should be fixed first.
- Today's priorities: the top high-impact actions to focus on now.
- Quick wins: low-effort actions that can create useful momentum.
- Missing features: CRM, automation, Marketplace, or workflow capabilities the user has not adopted yet.

Each recommendation can include:

- A clear title.
- Impact level.
- Effort level.
- A short reason.
- Optional target or goal connection.
- Suggested subtasks.
- Marketplace setup links where relevant.
- Source and trust signals.
- Feedback and learning notes when prior feedback affects ranking.


Readiness checklist

AI Coach checks whether the workspace is ready before it generates recommendations.

Readiness can include:

- AI Coach installed for the workspace.
- AI Coach enabled for the current user.
- Shared company context completed.
- Product or offer context completed.
- Personal Coach Brief completed.
- AI service availability.
- At least one ready advice skill when strategic business recommendations are expected.

If the workspace is not ready, AI Coach explains what is missing instead of giving weak or generic advice.


Coach Brief and strategy profile

AI Coach becomes more useful when each user completes their Coach Brief and strategy profile.

The strategy profile can include:

- Target market focus.
- Ideal customer profile.
- Offer angle.
- Segment focus.
- Sales motion.
- Deal movement strategy.
- Outreach posture.
- Positioning notes.
- Market view.
- Strategy hypothesis.
- Draft tone and voice preferences.
- CTA style, formality level, and reading level.

This allows recommendations and AI outputs across the system to better match the customer's actual go-to-market approach.


Idea validation

AI Coach supports idea validation context for early-stage or strategy-focused users.

Users can capture:

- Value proposition.
- Target market.
- Customer pain points.
- Assumptions to test.
- Competitors.
- Differentiator.

This helps AI Coach recommend validation experiments and avoid pushing a business to scale before its assumptions are clear.


Lean Canvas support

AI Coach can use Lean Canvas as a structured business model layer.

When Lean Canvas is enabled, AI Coach can reference:

- Problem.
- Customer segments.
- Unique value proposition.
- Solution.
- Channels.
- Revenue streams.
- Cost structure.
- Key metrics.
- Unfair advantage.
- Completion status and missing blocks.

This makes recommendations more grounded for founders, operators, and teams still clarifying their business model.


Marketplace guidance

AI Coach integrates with the workspace Marketplace.

It can recommend installing or completing relevant modules when the workspace needs more capability. For example, if a recommendation depends on a missing channel, automation, strategy, marketing, or setup skill, AI Coach can point the user toward the right Marketplace action.

This helps customers understand not only what to do, but what capability they need in order to do it well.


Task follow-through

AI Coach recommendations can become actionable task candidates.

Task candidates can preserve:

- The recommendation source.
- The guidance run.
- The target or goal relationship.
- Suggested subtasks.
- Marketplace skill source.
- Follow-through context.

This turns coaching into execution rather than leaving advice as a passive suggestion.


Trust and explanation signals

AI Coach recommendation cards can explain why a recommendation appeared.

Signals can include:

- Confidence.
- Context quality.
- Goal relevance.
- Source section.
- Marketplace source.
- Setup gap.
- Lean Canvas context.
- Ready skill coverage.

These signals help users understand whether a recommendation is based on strong context, setup gaps, goals, or installed capabilities.


Feedback-aware learning

Users and admins can provide feedback on recommendation quality.

Feedback can include signals such as:

- Useful.
- Not relevant.
- Already done.
- Show fewer like this.
- Dismissed.
- Acted on.

AI Coach can use explicit feedback conservatively to adjust future ranking and explain when feedback influenced a recommendation.


Diagnostics and admin insight

Admins can review AI Coach recommendation patterns through diagnostics.

This helps teams see:

- Which recommendations users accept.
- Which recommendations are dismissed.
- Which recommendation types need review.
- Which patterns are useful or not relevant.
- Where tuning controls or setup changes may improve the experience.


Guidance modes

AI Coach supports different guidance modes for different customer needs.

Foundation Mode

Best for early setup, founders, and teams that need business fundamentals reviewed. This mode can surface foundation gaps around company setup, product definitions, pricing, offer clarity, sales process, segmentation, budget, targets, and go-to-market basics.

Operations Mode

Best for teams that already have their business model and strategy in place. This mode focuses on execution inside the growth system, such as activities, follow-ups, automations, tasks, deals, targets, and feature adoption.

Guardian Mode

Best for teams that want minimal proactive coaching. In this mode, AI Coach avoids daily coaching and only alerts for critical issues.

Response: "AI Coach is designed as an orchestrator. It uses ready workspace skills and CRM evidence. If the right context or advice skill is missing, it recommends setup instead of pretending it has enough information."
